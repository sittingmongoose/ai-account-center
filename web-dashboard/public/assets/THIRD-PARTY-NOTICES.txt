Third-party artwork, icons and fonts in the AI Account Center dashboard

The dashboard (the Leptos app in web-dashboard/leptos) embeds or serves the files below.
Product names and marks remain their respective owners' trademarks; they identify the
corresponding services only.

Libraries
- Leptos 0.9 (https://leptos.dev) and wasm-bindgen, js-sys and web-sys
  (https://github.com/wasm-bindgen/wasm-bindgen): MIT or Apache-2.0, compiled into the
  WebAssembly runtime.

Fonts
- Instrument Sans: Copyright 2022 The Instrument Sans Project Authors
  (https://github.com/Instrument/instrument-sans). SIL Open Font License 1.1, full text in
  public/assets/OFL-InstrumentSans.txt. Shipped as public/assets/InstrumentSans.ttf (width axis
  pinned at 100, subset to Latin).
- Martian Mono: Copyright 2021 The Martian Mono Project Authors (https://github.com/evilmartians/mono).
  SIL Open Font License 1.1, full text in web-dashboard/leptos/fonts/OFL-MartianMono.txt (served at
  /app/OFL-MartianMono.txt). Shipped as MartianMono.ttf (width axis pinned at 87.5, subset to Latin).

Icons (web-dashboard/leptos/src/ui/icons.rs)
- Lucide 0.460.0 icon geometry (https://lucide.dev), ISC License, full text in
  web-dashboard/leptos/src/ui/LICENSE-lucide.txt. Portions are from Feather (MIT), Copyright (c)
  2013-2022 Cole Bemis.

Platform glyphs (web-dashboard/leptos/src/ui/marks/platform-*.svg)
- Apple, Windows and Ubuntu glyphs from Simple Icons 16.33.0 (https://simpleicons.org), CC0 1.0,
  licence in web-dashboard/leptos/src/ui/marks/simple-icons-LICENSE.md. Used only as platform indicators.

Provider marks (web-dashboard/leptos/src/ui/marks)
Official artwork, unmodified apart from the crop recorded in leptos/src/ui/marks/sources.json, which
also lists each source URL and SHA-256 and the optical scale the dashboard applies:
- Claude Spark (Anthropic press kit), Codex: OpenAI Blossom (OpenAI brand pack), Google Antigravity,
  Cursor 2D cube (Cursor brand assets), Muse Code: Meta mark, Kimi Code app icon (Moonshot AI,
  downscaled to 128 px with its own plate), Qwen symbol (Alibaba Qwen), Z.ai, OpenCode Go.

Historical credit
- From 2026-10 to 2026-11-14 Home, Analytics and Accounts & Settings were drawn with Slint 1.18.1
  (https://slint.dev) under its Royalty-free license; the Slint UI and its embedded copies of these
  fonts and of Inter were removed with it.

OpenCode
